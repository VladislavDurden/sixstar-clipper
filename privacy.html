Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

sixstar-clipper ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel "GTA 6 Stream Moments". It is not offered to other users.

What data the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the owner's own channel (scope youtube.upload) and to read basic information about that channel (scope youtube.readonly), for example its name, to confirm the correct channel is connected.

What data the app stores

The OAuth access token is stored only on the owner's own computer, in a file readable only by the owner. The app keeps a local list of the IDs of videos it uploaded, to avoid duplicates. No data about any other person or channel is collected.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer any Google user data to third parties. Data is used only to upload videos to the owner's channel. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token file also removes the app's access.

Contact

Questions: via the "About" section of the YouTube channel.
