sixstar-clipper

sixstar-clipper is a private tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel GTA 6 Stream Moments to prepare and upload short vertical highlight videos (YouTube Shorts) from GTA 6 Twitch streams. Every video credits the original streamer.

The tool is not offered to the public. It accesses only the channel owner's own YouTube channel.
